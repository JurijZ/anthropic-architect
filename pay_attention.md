* System decomposition, this comes up a lot and is worth being genuinely comfortable with;
* AI governance and how to apply it in practice, not just define it;
* Architectural tradeoffs specific to highly secure or regulated environments;
* Claude.md usage vs system prompt;
* Terminology, like triage, 



The trap to watch out for:
A few questions are structured in a sneaky way. They’ll ask something like “what would you do at this step of the process” and then follow up with “what would you do before this process.” It’s easy to lose track of which process they’re actually asking you to evaluate your options against, read the setup line twice before you commit to an answer.
